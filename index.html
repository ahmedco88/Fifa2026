About & how to use

Your pocket guide to the World Cup 2026: all 104 matches, live scores, and your teams in one place. Here's what each part does:

📅ScheduleEvery match, grouped by day. Kick-off times are shown in your local time automatically.
📊StandingsTap the Standings tab for the group tables: wins, draws, losses, goal difference and points, updated as results come in.
👟ScorersTap the Scorers tab for the tournament's top goalscorers, updated live as goals go in (own goals aren't counted).
🏆BracketTap the 🏆 Bracket button on the Schedule to see the knockout rounds, who's playing who, and who goes through.
🏅HistoryTap the History tab for past World Cups (1930 to 2022): hosts, winners, and Golden Boot top scorers.
🔍Filter by groupTap a group (A to L) to show only those matches.
⭐My teamsTap ⚙ Choose my teams to pick your favourites. They get highlighted, and ★ My teams filters to just them.
🔔Match remindersTap the 🔔 on any upcoming match to be reminded 15 min before kick-off, only the matches you pick.
⏱️Upcoming onlyHide matches that have already finished.
📍Today's matchesJump straight to today's games.
📆Add to calendarTap Add on any match to save it to your phone or computer's calendar.
🌙Dark modeTap the moon / sun (top) to switch themes. Your choice is remembered.
🔄Live scoresScores update on their own while matches are being played. You can also tap Refresh any time.

Times may lag a minute or two behind the live broadcast. Schedule from the open-source openfootball project; live scores via ESPN's public feed.

📱 On iPhone: tap the Share icon, then Add to Home Screen to install this app.
