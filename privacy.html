WorldCup Tracker 2026

Privacy Policy · Hamada Digital

Effective date: 10 June 2026  ·  Last updated: 30 June 2026

WorldCup Tracker 2026 ("the App") is developed and operated by Hamada Digital, a personal developer account operated by Ahmed Al-Obaidi, based in Victoria, Australia. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details to use it.

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your selected favourite teams (stored in your browser's localStorage)
  • Your notification preferences (stored in your browser's localStorage)

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser/app storage.

2. Match Schedule Data

The App fetches publicly available FIFA World Cup 2026 match schedule and score data from the open-source openfootball/worldcup.json project hosted on GitHub. This is a public, anonymous data fetch — no personal information is sent as part of this request.

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services:

  • GitHub Pages — static file hosting (used for the Google Play app and the github.io web address). GitHub may log standard server access logs (IP address, browser type). See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare Pages: static file hosting (used for the pages.dev web address shared directly with users). As with any web host, Cloudflare may log standard server access logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the page. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — a privacy-first, cookieless analytics service used to count aggregate page views and understand general usage (e.g. number of visits, referring sites, country, browser type). It does not use cookies, does not store your IP address, does not fingerprint you, and does not track you across other websites or over time. No individual user can be identified. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Visitor counter (Cloudflare Worker) — the footer shows a public count of how many people have used the App. This is a single anonymous total stored on Cloudflare; your browser is counted once. No cookies, no personal data, and no IP address are stored for this counter.

No advertising, behavioural tracking, or crash-reporting services are used.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies and does not track you as an individual. The only usage data collected is anonymous, aggregate page-view statistics via Cloudflare Web Analytics (see section 3) — this cannot be tied to you and is not used to follow you across sites or over time.

5. Notifications

If you enable match reminders, the App uses your device's local notification system only. No data is sent to external servers to deliver notifications. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since no personal information is collected from any user, the App is safe for all ages.

7. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data at risk. Locally stored preferences (favourite teams) remain on your device under the security controls of your own operating system and browser.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete. To remove locally stored preferences, simply clear your browser or app storage.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
